# Reality cards

Read this reference for the `reality` command. A card connects one learning
topic to one target role. It explains a practical task, likely failure modes,
and how strongly the job relevance is supported.

## Evidence rules

- Prefer primary sources: official product or platform documentation, an
  employer's own job description, or a first-hand engineering account.
- Cite each claim near the claim and save working source links in the card.
- Label relevance as **widely agreed**, **contested**, or **anecdotal**. These
  labels describe the evidence reviewed; they are not statistical confidence.
- Treat interview patterns as company- and level-dependent. Compare them with
  current target-role job descriptions when available.
- Separate a documented production behavior from a hypothetical failure
  example. Never describe an invented incident as real.
- If evidence is unavailable or conflicts, say so and leave the card
  unverified. Do not fill a weak spot with plausible-sounding industry lore.

## Card fields

For each topic and role, record:

- **Real task:** a concrete work task, with the sequence of decisions or steps
- **Production failure:** a documented or clearly marked hypothetical failure
  mode, its impact, and the mitigation
- **Filter or daily use:** Interview filter, Daily use, Both, or Unclear
- **Evidence label:** Widely agreed, Contested, or Anecdotal, with a short reason
- **Job-description phrases:** exact short phrases from supplied or cited JDs
- **Smallest realistic exercise:** a short task that uses realistic data and
  tests the stated concept
- **Sources:** working links with publisher and date when available

Keep the first read compact. Put secondary evidence in an expandable section
when the destination supports it.
