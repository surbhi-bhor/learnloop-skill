# Review schedule

Read this reference for the `review` command. Reviews ask for recall before
showing a note or answer. Keep review intervals simple and bring weak spots
back sooner.

## Regular topics

Schedule the first review one day after learning. When the learner passes a
style-free recall or teach-back, schedule the next review one week later; after
that pass, schedule one month later. After a successful one-month review, stop
the default schedule. The learner can request more review or set a new date.

If the learner needs work, show the missing point, practice it, and set the
next review for the following day. Do not move a due date forward simply
because the learner opened the note.

## Weak spots

Set the first delayed teach-back one day after flagging. If it does not pass,
keep the weak spot open and schedule another attempt after 3 days; after the
second failed attempt use 7 days, then 14 days for later failures. A pass after
the delay changes `Delayed teach-back` to Passed, sets `Status` to Resolved,
records `Resolved on`, and clears `Next review`.

An immediate correction or retry can teach the missing point, but it does not
resolve the weak spot. A delayed attempt must still pass.

## Updating records

- On a completed topic review, update `Last reviewed`, `Review count`, `Next
  review`, `Teach-back result`, and the linked Practice Bank attempt.
- On a weak-spot attempt, update `Last review`, `Review count`, and `Next
  review`; update `Delayed teach-back`, `Status`, and `Resolved on` only after a
  delayed pass.
- Use the `Due today` and `Open weak spots` views to choose work. Do not expose
  the answer until the learner has attempted recall.
- A missed review is still due; keep it visible until completed or until the
  learner changes its date.
